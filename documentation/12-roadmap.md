# Product Roadmap

The roadmap is intentionally incremental. Each phase should strengthen the core learning loop before adding breadth.

## Phase 0: Feasibility and Validation

**Goal:** Prove theory correctness, audio behavior, mobile instrument interaction, and educational value.

**Deliverables:**

- Theory and audio POC.
- Responsive fretboard POC.
- Shared piano and guitar state.
- One learning comparison flow.
- Feedback from three testers spanning newcomer, intermediate guitarist, and keyboard/producer profiles.

## Phase 1: MVP Release

**Goal:** Deliver a coherent self-guided exploration tool.

**Deliverables:**

- Supported root and mode selection.
- Piano and guitar exploration.
- Interval-role explanations.
- Drone and pedal contexts.
- Minimal Ear Gym.
- Responsive and accessible core flow.
- Local preferences and best-effort persisted streak.
- Local, privacy-safe diagnostic logging and user-triggered JSONL export.

## Phase 2: Guided Comparison

**Goal:** Turn free exploration into repeatable learning journeys.

**Potential capabilities:**

- Side-by-side mode comparator.
- Short guided discovery lessons.
- More explicit transfer exercises across roots.
- Local progress history.
- Teacher-friendly lesson links without requiring accounts.

## Phase 3: Harmonic Context

**Goal:** Connect scale choices to chord context and composition.

**Potential capabilities:**

- Procedural drone and backing patterns.
- Chord progression context.
- Chord-tone and tension explanations.
- Simple melody challenges.
- Audio export if technical and licensing constraints are acceptable.

## Phase 4: Expansion

**Goal:** Expand reach only after the learning model is working.

**Potential capabilities:**

- Additional instruments.
- Alternate and custom guitar tunings, such as Drop D, DADGAD, and open tunings.
- Optional accounts and cross-device progress.
- Curated song examples with appropriate rights.
- Educator tools.
- Community sharing.

## Prioritization Rules

Prioritize features that:

1. Improve the learner's ability to hear and explain a difference.
2. Increase transfer between roots or instruments.
3. Work on small touch devices.
4. Reduce dependence on passive content.
5. Can be tested without introducing account or backend complexity.

Defer features that primarily add catalog size, social mechanics, or visual polish without improving understanding.
