# Product Vision

ScaleScape helps curious learners at any experience level understand scales and modes as musical sounds and interval relationships instead of memorizing disconnected instrument patterns.

## Problem

Many music learners can find scale shapes but cannot explain or hear why those shapes work. Existing tools often behave as static atlases:

- They show dots without explaining interval function.
- They make audio optional instead of central.
- They focus on one instrument at a time.
- They do not connect a mode's formula to its musical character.
- They provide little support for transferring knowledge between instruments.

This creates a gap between theoretical naming, visual memorization, and practical musical use.

## Product Promise

After a short session, a learner should be able to say and demonstrate, even if they did not know the theory vocabulary beforehand:

> This mode sounds different because of these characteristic intervals, and I can find and use them on my instrument.

## Core Learning Loop

```text
Listen -> Recognize -> Locate -> Play -> Contextualize -> Listen again
```

The product should introduce sound before exposing too much visual information. Visualizations explain what the learner hears; they should not replace listening.

## Product Principles

| Principle | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Audio before abstraction | Play the scale in a meaningful harmonic context before presenting all of its theory. |
| Interaction is the teacher | Understanding comes from manipulating sound and seeing the result. Text only names what the learner just experienced; it never carries the lesson by itself. |
| Plain first, depth on demand | Start with familiar language and one action. Reveal interval names, formulas, and advanced detail only when the learner asks or the interaction makes them useful. |
| Intervals over dots | Every highlighted note should have a musical function, not only a position. |
| Compare nearby concepts | Differences between related modes are more useful than isolated definitions. |
| Cross-instrument by default | The same scale should be understandable on piano and guitar. |
| Small screens first | The core loop is designed for a phone viewport and scales up to desktop, never the reverse. |
| Small sessions, real insight | A learner should gain one concrete understanding in three minutes. |
| Explore without fear | The MVP should allow experimentation without grading every action. |
| Accessibility is part of the model | Sound, color, touch, and notation must have equivalent paths where possible. |

## Differentiation

ScaleScape is not positioned as a comprehensive course, a performance grader, or a generic fretboard chart. Its initial differentiation is:

1. Audio-first exploration with drone and harmonic context.
2. Interval roles such as tonic, third, fifth, and characteristic degree.
3. Synchronized piano and guitar representations.
4. Short recognition challenges that test whether the learner can hear the difference.

## Competitive Context

The categories below are planning references, not an exhaustive market analysis:

| Category | Examples | What they do well | Typical gap ScaleScape addresses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reference and chart tools | musictheory.net, Teoria, Guitar Scientist | Broad catalogs and clear diagrams | Exploration is visual-first; comparing related modes and transferring across instruments is not the core loop. |
| Ear-training apps | Functional Ear Trainer, EarMaster | Structured, contextual listening practice | Fixed curricula; listening is not connected to free instrument exploration. |
| Producer-oriented theory | Hooktheory | Strong harmonic context for songwriting | Not focused on locating intervals on an instrument. |

ScaleScape's hypothesis is that combining audio-first comparison, interval roles, and synchronized instruments in short sessions serves a need these categories leave open.

## Success Hypothesis

If learners can identify a meaningful difference between related modes after a three-minute interaction, then connect that difference to an interval with the app's guidance, they will perceive ScaleScape as more useful than a static scale chart.

## Non-Goals for the Initial Product

- Replacing an instrumental teacher.
- Measuring detailed performance accuracy.
- Supporting every instrument and every scale at launch.
- Teaching full harmony, notation, and composition in the first release.
- Building a social or subscription platform before the learning loop is validated.
